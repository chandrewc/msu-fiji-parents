Parents Fund

Parents see the difference Sigma Mu makes in their son’s life. This annual fund turns that connection into practical support for the member experience.

  • House improvements and comfortable study spaces
  • Academic awards, tutoring, and learning resources
  • Leadership development and member wellness
  • Family Weekend and opportunities to connect

Families are invited to give throughout the year. Chapter leadership directs gifts toward current member needs.
